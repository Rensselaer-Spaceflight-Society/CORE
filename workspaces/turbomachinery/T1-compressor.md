# T1 · Find the exact compressor and its map

[Team page](README.md) · [Meeting introduction](../meeting-1.md) · [Candidate review](../../docs/project/dp2-review.md)

**Time:** 30 minutes, up to 45 with help. **Review role:** Turbomachinery lead. No prior experience required; ask for a five-minute worked example before starting.

## Do this today

Find the supplier/part number, or write NOT FOUND. Open the Garrett reference linked in the DP-2 review. Label its flow axis, pressure-ratio axis and speed lines. Explain why a same-diameter billet wheel may not share that map.

**Done means:** A source link plus a three-row table: exact part, map reference conditions, one unresolved question.

**Optional stretch:** With the lead, convert 0.30 kg/s to lb/min; distinguish unit conversion from corrected flow.

## Keep for later

Map matching, surge/choke margins and CFD are later work. Do not certify the 66 krpm point or extrapolate an idle map today.

**Handoff:** T2 and C5 need the part/map identity.
