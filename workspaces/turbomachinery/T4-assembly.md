# T4 · Inventory existing CAD without redrawing it

[Team page](README.md) · [Meeting introduction](../meeting-1.md) · [Candidate review](../../docs/project/dp2-review.md)

**Time:** 30 minutes, up to 45 with help. **Review role:** Turbomachinery lead; Structures lead for interfaces. No prior experience required; ask for a five-minute worked example before starting.

## Do this today

List up to three available models/documents, their revision/location and one stated dimension with units. If CAD is unavailable, use the candidate casing and wheel table as a document inventory and mark the actual files NOT FOUND.

**Done means:** Three inventory rows labeled available, missing or needs review.

**Optional stretch:** Sketch a rough assembly and identify one mating surface to ask Structures about.

## Keep for later

Do not scrap or overwrite CAD, freeze interfaces or mistake outer casing diameter for turbine tip clearance.

**Handoff:** Chief engineer maintains configuration; S1/S2 review shaft and inlet interfaces.
