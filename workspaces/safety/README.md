**Lead role:** Safety lead. Safety is a review function across every sub-team; a newcomer is not being delegated authority to approve a test or material.

## First meeting

- Review the shared [T5 / SAFE1 rotor-energy card](../turbomachinery/T5-rotor-energy.md). It is one output by the dual-role participant, not two workloads. Its [guided Python worksheet](../python/turbomachinery/t5_rotor_energy.py) works through stored rotor energy and asks for two facts a containment review would still need.
- Ask each team for one uncertainty or possible failure and record it below.
- Explain the difference between a paper exercise, a supervised bench activity and an approved engine/rotor test.
- Identify the responsible institutional/shop authority and questions about the approval process. The Safety lead handles contacts; members do not need to discover or negotiate the process alone today.

| Team / code | Question or hazard to investigate | Evidence needed | Review role | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T5 / SAFE1 | Estimated rotor inertia and local fragment scenario | CAD revision and reviewed mechanical analysis | Safety + Structures | Open |
| C3 / C7 | Fuel/start-method test prerequisites | Procedure, hardware and institutional requirements | Safety + Combustion & Systems | Open |
| S1 | Exact bearing and later rotor-test suitability | Supplier limits, loads and reviewed rig plan | Safety + Structures | Open |

The Safety lead should seek a willing additional reviewer/helper as the work grows. Do not silently dual-assign another newcomer or publish personal participation preferences. Keep those discussions with the facilitator.
