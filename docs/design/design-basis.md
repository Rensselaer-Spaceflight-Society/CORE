# PD-1 design basis

PRELIMINARY - NOT FOR MANUFACTURE. Case file: [`config/cases/pd1-jm85.yaml`](../../config/cases/pd1-jm85.yaml).
Every number below is generated by `python run_case.py pd1-jm85 --robustness`; the tables in
[`results/pd1-jm85/SUMMARY.md`](results/pd1-jm85/SUMMARY.md) are the authoritative copy for the snapshot revision.

## 1. Objective and what PD-1 is

Objective: a **self-sustaining** student turbojet within a $5,500 target / $6,000 ceiling; thrust secondary.

PD-1 is a fixed-geometry candidate assembled from purchased rotating parts and CORE-made static parts:

| Element | PD-1 choice | Evidence class |
|---|---|---|
| Compressor wheel | GT3076R-class 57.04 / 76.13 mm, 56 trim (Kinugawa billet 11+0 listing; stock Garrett 452708-0001 is the mapped alternative) | supplied dimensions; **map is a related-wheel proxy** |
| Compressor characteristic | Garrett GT3076R published map, digitized (7 speed lines, efficiency islands 60–77 %) | reference + digitization uncertainty |
| Turbine wheel | JETMAX 85 mm cast wheel, finished and prebalanced, Ø9.99 mm bore | supplied drawing; blade angles/throat/alloy **unresolved** |
| NGV | JETMAX 85 mm casting (blank; CORE finishes shroud/flange) | supplied envelope; **throat and exit angle assumed** (65°, range 58–72°); the blank's Ø48 hub pocket is **blind** (centre web + boss), so the rear-bearing path through the hub is an open decision |
| Diffuser | CORE vaned channel diffuser, 19 vanes, LE Ø82.2 mm, exit Ø126 mm, width 6 → 9 mm | design + model (`core/diffuser.py`) |
| Combustor | Front-dome annular combustor with 8 J-vaporizers in the DP-2 152.4 mm casing; sized at 68 krpm with a 2.5 % prescribed loss and 1.4 ms bulk residence time | model (`core/combustor.py` via M20) |
| Nozzle | Conical convergent, exit Ø54 mm, tailcone | design choice from the match |
| Shaft/bearings | Stepped 4140 shaft (6 mm compressor seat, 10 mm journals, 13 mm body); 10 × 26 × 8 hybrid angular-contact bearings both ends; soft damped (elastomer) mounts; front bearing recessed 6 mm into the backplate | design; support properties **assumed** |
| Speeds | steady range 56–68 krpm (map-supported low end to max continuous); design/sizing point 68 krpm; DP-2's 66 krpm inside the range | decision |

**Architecture note.** The repository calls the combustor "reverse-flow". The library geometry, and PD-1, is a
front-dome annular combustor: air flows aft in both annuli and enters through liner holes; the gas flows aft
into the NGV; J-shaped vaporizers reverse the fuel–air mixture toward the dome. If the team intends a folded
combustor wrapped around the turbine, the axial stack changes completely.

## 2. Why PD-1 departs from the DP-2 numbers

| DP-2 proposal | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0.30 kg/s at PR 1.63, 66 krpm | Not supported by the proxy map: at 66 krpm the 76 mm wheel chokes at ~0.25 kg/s and PR ~1.29; 0.30 kg/s at PR ≥ 1.63 first appears near 85 krpm (η ≈ 0.70) | `results/dp2-prescribed/map_consistency.json`; `test_map_points_reproduce_digitized_lines_and_are_physical` |
| same | Supporting check (corrected 2026-10-04): PR 1.63 at 66 krpm needs a blade (Euler) work coefficient of 0.837 against a Stanitz slip of 0.820 for the 11-blade wheel (no inlet swirl, η_c 0.72). It still exceeds slip, but only while η_c < 0.735, so the map evidence above carries the conclusion. (The earlier 0.905 applied the power-input factor the wrong way.) | M10 fixed-wheel mode in the DP-2 screen |
| 88.2 / 53.9 mm turbine | Closest purchasable documented wheel is JETMAX 85 / 55 mm (TW85); its 9.99 mm bore sets a 10 mm journal | supplier drawing |
| 8 mm bearings (708) | 10 × 26 × 8 at both ends (bore change, stiffer body) | assembly checks |
| 4 % (combustor) vs 6 % (cycle) loss | At PD-1 flow both 4 % and 6 % fail the dilution-penetration screen (and 6 %/2 ms is infeasible); 2.5 % / 1.4 ms passes every existing combustor screen and shortens the bearing span | `combustor.json → basis_comparison` |
| Imposed turbine work | Replaced by fixed-geometry matching; work comes from the rated stage | `core/engine_match.py` |

The DP-2 cycle arithmetic itself is reproduced exactly by the new prescribed-flow mode (T03 348.10 K,
P03 163.51 kPa, 23.72 kg/h, 98.1 N); the issue is the operating point, not the arithmetic.

## 5. Assumptions that carry the result

Full list with sources: `results/pd1-jm85/assumptions_register.json` and `seed_provenance.json`.
The ones that matter most (robustness rank):

1. NGV exit angle / throat area (assumed 65° → 1494 mm²) and rotor exit angle (−58° → 1748 mm²).
2. Combustor actual loss (2.5 % basis at the design flow function; 4–8 % evaluated).
3. Ambient temperature (hot day +57 K T04).
4. Turbine loss level (Soderberg extrapolated to rotor Re 1.6–2.0e4 over 56–68 krpm, below the code's 2e4 validity flag at 56–66 krpm; ×1.5 and ×2 evaluated, plus the loss-multiplier threshold) and compressor efficiency of the billet wheel/vaned diffuser (−0.04 evaluated).
5. Turn/deswirl loss 2 % (K 0.5 with deswirl vanes), tunnel leakage 2 %, inlet loss 1 %.
6. Soft bearing-mount stiffness 2 N/µm and damping 1000 N·s/m (rotordynamics).
7. Metal temperatures for clearances (rotor relative total − 30 K; shroud 0.85 T41 + 0.15 T03).
8. Smaller cycle inputs, all case-file assumptions (now listed in `assumptions_register.json` too): nozzle
   Cd 0.97 and Cv 0.97 (their product acts on capacity — see the audit), jet-pipe loss 1 %, combustion
   efficiency 0.96, fuel LHV 43 MJ/kg, disc windage moment coefficient 0.004, bearing friction f0 1.7 at
   8 cSt.
