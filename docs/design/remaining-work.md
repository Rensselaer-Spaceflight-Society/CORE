# Remaining work register (prioritized)

Each item names the consequence, the input that resolves it, the components affected and who would normally
supply it. Order = impact on the self-sustain decision and on what can be machined first. Updated after the
independent verification of 2026-10-04 ([`independent-verification.md`](independent-verification.md)); items
marked **new** came from that pass.

| # | Item | Why it matters | Required input (measurement / analysis / supplier answer) | Affects | Owner (role) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **NGV85 and TW85 throat areas, blade/vane counts, blade handedness** | The match is most sensitive to these (assumed NGV exit angle 58–72° moves T04 by about ±100 K); rotation direction must agree with the compressor | Buy/borrow the castings; pin-gauge throat opening at hub/mid/tip on every passage; count; record rotation viewed from the front. Or ask JETMAX for throat area and design point | NG-01, TR-01, nozzle size, CAD of vanes | Turbomachinery |
| 2 | **NGV hub web and rear-bearing support path** (new) | The blank's Ø48 pocket is blind (11 mm deep, then a centre web and a conical boss reaching into the rotor's shroud section). The stack's rear spacer, turbine boss and carrier pass through that zone, so the carrier location, the assembly order and the rear support stiffness/temperature are not established | Team decision between: bore the web and keep the pocket as the carrier seat; remove the web (Ø48 through); a separate rear housing; or a carrier flanged to the machined web. Then a machining plan and a strength/stiffness check of what remains (hub ring 3.7 mm wall + vanes) | NG-01, BC-02, TU-01, SH-01, rotordynamics, balancing, assembly order | Structures + Turbomachinery |
| 3 | **Turbine stage efficiency at Re ≈ 1.6–2.0e4** (new) | After the viscosity correction the rotor Reynolds number is below the code's 2e4 validity flag at 56–66 krpm and only 0.1 % above it at 68 krpm. The predicted η_tt ≈ 0.85 comes from Soderberg (basis Re 1e5) and probably is optimistic; the 2e4 flag itself has no cited source | Measured stage data (cold-flow turbine rig or engine data), or a low-Re loss correlation with a stated validity range; team decision on the flag threshold and on the validity taxonomy (unresolved vs fail) | TR-01, NG-01, matching, robustness | Turbomachinery |
| 4 | **Compressor wheel identity and characteristic** | Map is a related-wheel proxy; billet 11+0 wheel listed sold out | Decide wheel (stock Garrett 452708-0001 matches the map); measure hub, length, tip profile and **bore**; if billet, obtain or test a characteristic | CW-01, CH-01, matching, compressor seat fit | Turbomachinery |
| 5 | **Bearing supplier data and soft-mount stiffness/damping** | Rotordynamic separation relies on ~2 N/µm supports with ~1000 N·s/m damping (assumed); the rigid modes are heavily damped only because of that damping; DN/limiting speed unknown at fuel/oil-mist lubrication | Supplier limiting speed and preload; measure O-ring mount stiffness and damping on a rig; modal tap test of the assembled rotor on its supports | BR-01/02, BC-01/02, SH-01, max speed | Structures |
| 6 | **Wheel fits** (new emphasis) | The TW85 bore 9.99 ±0.005 mm on a 10.000 mm seat is a 5–15 µm interference; the compressor's 6 mm seat is only an envelope for a 5.99 mm bore. | 7 | **Turbine wheel alloy, speed rating, life** | No published alloy/speed rating; tip speed 303 m/s at 68 krpm; blade sections assumed | Supplier statement (alloy, heat treatment, rated speed); blade/disc stress analysis from a scan; inspection after finishing | TR-01, max speed | Structures + Safety |
| 8 | **Combustor behaviour and its sizing model** | Sized at a prescribed 2.5 % / 1.4 ms with 8 J-vaporizers; ignition, lean stability, vaporizer coking and exit pattern are unmodelled; P03 only ~1.5 bar. **New:** with the uniform-liner-static assumption removed, a momentum balance puts the sized holes at ≈ 5 % loss; the dilution-penetration pass depends on two offsetting definitions (≈ 0.89 with Lefebvre's) | Cold-flow split and pressure-drop test of the liner; ignition/stability rig or first-light plan; combustor owner to revisit the liner static model and the penetration definitions; thermal analysis | CB-01..05, VP-01, fuel schedule, cycle | Combustion & Systems |
| 9 | **Liner fabrication route vs weld seam** | Dense dilution rows leave no 3 mm seam band on rolled-sheet liners (arc pitch 9.8/9.5 mm vs ~11.6 mm needed; at best 1 hole on the seam) | Shop answer on seam width; or drawn tube (4 in × 0.065 in for the outer liner) and rerate at stock diameter | CB-02/03 | Combustion & Systems + shop |
| 10 | **Low-speed and starting region** | Map begins ~54.5 krpm physical; light-off, assisted acceleration, starter cut-out and idle are not established; starter power bound extrapolated; starter peak current check fails (142 A vs 66 A) | Motoring tests of the assembled core (power vs speed); starter and battery selection after that; transient model only once low-speed data exist | ST-01, controls, electrical | Combustion & Systems |

Continuation note: the candidate run takes about 20 minutes with `--robustness`. Run it, then the DP-2 and
baseline cases, then `python scripts/make_design_snapshot.py`. To bring in a measured NGV throat, set
`throat_area` in `data/components/ngv_jetmax_ngv85.yaml` (basis `supplied`, with source) — `stage_from_records`
then uses the measured area instead of the assumed angle and the nozzle trim table in the run output shows the
matching nozzle. The independent checks in `docs/design/checks/independent/` rerun against the new snapshot.
